What happened?

A RELLIS-3D user loaded Velodyne points into an example configured for the Ouster. The maintainer confirmed which sensor the calibration belongs to and supplied the transform between the two LiDARs. Both modes here keep the same image, scan, intrinsics and lens distortion. Only the Velodyne-to-Ouster transform changes.

The reference is the authors’ published frame. The excerpt comes from their separate merged ROS bag and pairs scans to camera images by timestamp. The two recordings have different timestamps. Remaining alignment differences can include motion during capture.
